Should this delivery-risk model be built?

You are the model reviewer. Turn an ambiguous request into a defensible prediction and evaluation contract. Every decision changes what the eventual system can honestly claim.

CASE FILE

Northstar delivery-risk advisor

Northstar wants to flag implementation projects likely to finish more than 24 hours late. A human operations team can review only 20% of new projects. Missing a truly late project costs much more than reviewing an on-time project, but too many alerts will overwhelm the team.

2,400completed projects
12%historically over 24 hours late
20%maximum review capacity

Available fields include planned integrations, document count, promised date, customer tier, account ID, actual completion timestamp, and the post-project delay code. Most records come from established enterprise customers; the next launch expands to smaller customers.

Your release gate

  • Pass both release-critical checks: leakage and production-shaped splitting.
  • Write three evidence notes explaining a normal case, a failure case, and limitations.
